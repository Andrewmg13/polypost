How it works

From one rough draft to a full campaign

Polypost is built on a multi-step AI workflow, not a single prompt. Each step does one job well, so every post is accurate, on-brand and written the way each platform expects.

The Polypost workflow, step by step

We designed and tested this pipeline in Dify, an open-source platform for building AI apps. Here is what happens after you click "Generate."

1

You give us the raw material

Paste a draft, add up to 10 files (press releases, PDFs, screenshots), describe your voice and tone, list the platforms you want, and choose a language.

2

A parameter extractor reads your request

An AI step turns plain English like "Twitter and LinkedIn" into a clean list of target platforms. If it can't find any, the workflow stops and asks you instead of guessing.

3

Files are sorted and read

Images and documents go down separate branches. A document extractor pulls the text out of PDFs and Word files so nothing important is left behind.

4

Everything is merged into one brief

An "Integrate Info" model combines your draft, the file contents and your tone into a single source of truth. Every post is written from this brief, so the numbers and claims always match.

5

Each platform gets its own writer

An iteration loop runs once per platform. First it identifies that platform's style (length, hashtags, emojis, formatting), then a second model writes the post in that style. Platforms run in parallel, so five posts take about as long as one.

6

A template packages the results

All posts are combined into one clean, labeled output that is ready to copy, schedule or send to your team for approval.

Built for more than social media

The same "one input → many tailored outputs" structure works anywhere a team rewrites one message for different audiences.

📊 Quarterly earnings, every audience

Input: the earnings release plus the CFO's talking points.

Outputs: an investor summary, an all-hands email for employees, a LinkedIn post for the company page, and a plain-language FAQ for customer-facing staff.

Why it matters: communications teams stop rewriting the same numbers under deadline, and every version stays consistent because it comes from one set of facts.

🚀 Product launches, every team

Input: an engineering release note or spec, with screenshots and design docs.

Outputs: help-center release notes, a sales one-pager with objection handling, a support brief with likely questions, and a partner email, in any regional language.

Why it matters: sales, support and marketing get accurate material the moment engineering finishes, which shortens launch time and cuts miscommunication.

Questions, answered

Will every post say the same thing?

The facts stay the same, but the writing doesn't. A tweet is short and conversational; a LinkedIn post tells a story and ends with a question; an Instagram caption leans on emojis and a call to action.

What if I forget to say which platforms I want?

The workflow checks for platforms before doing anything else. If it can't find any, it tells you right away instead of producing generic content.

Can I edit the posts before publishing?

Yes. Polypost gives you drafts, not auto-posts. Copy, tweak and publish them on your own schedule, or send them for approval on the Agency plan.

Is my content used to train AI models?

No. Your drafts and files are only used to generate your posts, and you can delete them at any time.
